# Aion  
 _Reclaim Your Time_

## The Problem
In today's hyper-connected world, our smartphones have become double-edged swords. While they offer infinite information, they also serve as the primary source of digital distraction, systematically stealing our productivity and focus.

### **The Distraction Epidemic**
*   **Average Screen Time:** The global average screen time has reached a staggering **6 hours and 36 minutes** per day.
*   **The "Refocus Tax":** Research shows it takes an average of **23 minutes and 15 seconds** to return to a state of "deep focus" after a single phone interruption.
*   **Cognitive Cost:** Frequent task-switching triggered by notifications can decrease overall productivity by up to **40%** and cause temporary IQ drops similar to sleep deprivation.
*   **Economic Impact:** The global economic cost of tech-related distractions is estimated at over **$1.3 trillion annually**.

Aion is a time-management application designed to help you combat these trends. By monitoring and controlling your app usage, Aion empowers you to set healthy boundaries and regain focus through intuitive design, gamification, and actionable insights.

## Main Goal
The primary objective of Aion is to empower users to become more conscious of their digital habits. By providing beautiful, intuitive data visualizations, customizable limits as well as gamification and online features, Aion helps reduce screen time and improve productivity without sacrificing aesthetic appeal.
